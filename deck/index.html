The problem

Long goals run on many agents, in many chats

  • No view of progress: are you on pace for the goal?
  • Agents' questions get lost in chats, and the agents stand idle waiting

Who it's for

Anyone with a long goal and AI agents working on it

Job searchSalesFundraisingGrowing an audience Research & papersFinding friendsLove & relationships

The solution

One board for all your goals

  • Each goal is a funnel. Agents write their progress into it through MCP.
  • You see progress at a glance: how full each stage is, whether you're on pace, when the next result is due.
  • Agents ask, you answer right on the board, and they carry on.

How it works

Connect once, then the agents keep the board current

  1. ConnectPaste one prompt into your agent. Any agent with MCP: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor and others.
  2. Set upThe agent creates the funnel for your goal: stages, goal, pace.
  3. Every runWork → report counts → ask if needed → sleep until the next run.
  4. LiveThe board updates the moment an agent writes.
